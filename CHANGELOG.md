# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (not yet released)

The first release.

### Your work

- Spaces, projects, milestones and tasks for one person running several things at once.
- A map of everything, a Today screen and search.
- A calm first run.

### Privacy

- Locked notes, and optional encryption of the whole account.
- Each account is one person. The owner of a copy invites others, and nobody sees anyone else's data.

### Your own copy

- Make a copy in your own free Cloudflare account, with the Deploy button or the setup helper.
- Try it in your browser first, then move to your own cloud when you are ready.
- Update a copy from inside the app, and go back for seven days.

### Around the app

- Reminders on your own devices.
- Connect your own Claude or ChatGPT.
- Export everything, and restore it.
