# Code of conduct

VEMI is a small project and we want it to stay a kind place to work in. This is the short version
of the idea behind the Contributor Covenant.

## What we ask

- Be kind. Assume the other person is trying to help.
- Disagree about the work, not the person.
- No harassment, insults, threats or unwelcome attention, in any form.
- Do not share other people's private information.
- Keep it on topic and keep it civil.

## Where this applies

Issues, comments and any other space for this project, and anywhere you speak for it.

## What happens

The maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues and block people who break these rules.
We will act fairly and try to say why.

## How to report a problem

Use GitHub's private report on this repository: open the Security tab at
https://github.com/anvildynamicsaustralia/vemi and choose "Report a vulnerability". Say it is a
conduct report. Only the maintainers see it. For a security bug, see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
