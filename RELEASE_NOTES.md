# Release notes

One section per version, newest first, headed `## <version>` exactly as `package.json` says it.
The section for the version being built goes into the release manifest as its notes.

## 0.2.0

Easier to use on a phone, and the things you reach for are where you reach for them.

- Tap a space on the map to open it. "N more" and "Show fewer" fold its project list.
- Rename a space: tap its name on its own page, choose Rename from its menu on the map or
  on its page, or change it in the space's settings.
- The space menu on the map can add a project straight into that space, and opens Settings.
- Add makes a task, a project or a space. Choose which at the top.
- On a phone, the top of each main screen no longer repeats the page's name, so your work
  starts higher up.
- The Account list on a phone reads at full strength, and the Close button is gone: Account
  is a place in the bar.
- The canvas fits a phone: spaces stack in one column, it opens fitted to the screen, the
  zoom tools no longer cover the first box, and Fit puts it back.
- One way back on each page: a space's own header, or a project's or task's trail. No more
  three trails stacked on top of each other.
- Today: a task typed with "today" stays on Today, a task due today shows in Coming up, a late
  Next up offers Tomorrow, and finishing a task says so with Undo.
- Add: a day typed in the title sets When. Search's and the palette's New project open Add on
  Project.
- Board: a card's menu moves it to another state, which is the way on a phone. Bulk changes
  keep what is selected, and the selection bar fits a phone.
- Projects: the whole row opens a project, and long names show in full on a phone. On a
  project page, a task opens its details. Decisions and risks no longer overlap their dates.
- Notes add on Enter. "# Heading" is a heading. A link must be a web address.
- Phone: the canvas is a stacked list you scroll and press; segmented choices stay on one row;
  project stages wrap; the back gesture closes an Account section.
- Swipe on a phone, as in its own lists: right to finish a task on Today or the board, left
  for Tomorrow, Today or Archive.
- Drag Today's tasks into your own order (hold to drag on a phone).
- Name a new space or project right where you choose one in Add: "+ New project".
- Reminders and repeats can be set from a task's own details, and its due date is the button
  that changes it.
- Names you can rename show a small pencil; on a phone, renaming has its own ✓ and ×.
- Milestones move earlier or later from their ⋯; a project moves to another space with its
  tasks; the archive's Select shows what is chosen and can select all.
- An empty Today says so and offers ways to fill it. "Add a task here" from the map returns
  to the map.
- Dark mode dims behind sheets instead of greying them. Tips can be told apart from fields and
  say how to dismiss them.
