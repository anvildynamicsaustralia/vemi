# Connect your AI

You can let your own Claude or ChatGPT work in your VEMI. You ask in chat, and it reads and changes your VEMI for you. VEMI itself makes no model calls and uses no AI service. The assistant is yours, and so is its bill, if it has one.

## What you need

Your own Claude or ChatGPT account that lets you add a connector. Each service decides which plans can, so check theirs.

## Steps

1. Open VEMI and go to Account, then AI.
2. Next to Connector address, press Copy.
3. Under Connector settings, open Claude or ChatGPT.
4. In Claude, go to Customize, then Connectors, and add a custom connector. Paste the address.
5. In ChatGPT, use the plus, then Add custom MCP server. Paste the address.
6. VEMI shows a screen saying the app wants to read and change your VEMI, and that it can't delete anything. Press Allow. Deny turns it down.

The app then shows in the AI section with when it was last used. Press Revoke to let it go.

## What it can do

- List your spaces, projects and what is on today.
- Search.
- Add tasks, projects and notes.
- Edit a task or a project's brief.
- Move a task, and reschedule it.
- Tick a task off.
- Pick for today: choose which tasks go on today.

## What it cannot do

- Delete anything.
- Archive anything.
- Read an account with full-account encryption on. The AI section says so, and shows no address.
- Read locked notes.

## Undo

Every change it makes is listed in the AI section, with the app's name and what it did. Press Undo on a change and the item is put back. Undo lasts 7 days. It is refused when the item has changed since, or was already put back.
