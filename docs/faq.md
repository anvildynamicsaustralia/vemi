# Questions

## What does it cost?

VEMI is free and open source. It runs on Cloudflare's free plan, so a copy costs nothing to run. See [Self-hosting](self-hosting.md).

## Who can see my data?

Whoever runs your copy runs the space that holds it, so they could read what is not locked. Locked notes and full-account encryption stop that. Other people on the copy see none of your data.

## Can I leave?

Yes. Export everything and keep the file, as in [Backups](backups.md). You can restore it into another copy.

## What if I lose my recovery key?

A device you have already opened your locked notes on can still open them. If every such device and the recovery key are gone, nobody can open your locked notes, including whoever runs the copy.

## Why no email?

VEMI sends no email at all. Reminders are push and in the app, as in [Reminders](reminders.md).

## Does VEMI use AI?

No. VEMI makes no model calls. You can connect your own Claude or ChatGPT, as in [Connect your AI](connect-your-ai.md).
