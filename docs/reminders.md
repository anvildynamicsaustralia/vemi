# Reminders

VEMI reminds you by push on your device and inside the app. It sends no email.

## Per device

Reminders are set per device. Turning them on in your phone's browser does not turn them on for your laptop. In Account, under Reminders, each device shows "Reminders are on here" or "Reminders are off here", with Turn on, Send a test and Turn off.

Nothing is on until first run asks. First run offers reminders once, and Not now leaves every one of them off.

## What you can choose

- **Morning summary.** What is on today, once a day, at a time you pick, or off.
- **Reminders on tasks.** A reminder on a task fires at the time you set. A time typed into Add, such as at 3pm, sets one.
- **Quiet hours.** Nothing comes through between the two times. The choices are 9 pm to 7 am, 10 pm to 6 am, or None.

## On an iPhone

Push on an iPhone needs VEMI on the home screen, and iOS 16.4 or later. In Safari, use Share, then Add to Home Screen, and open VEMI from there. Then turn reminders on.

## If it does not work

If a browser has blocked reminders, allow them in its site settings and try again. In Try it now mode, reminders show only inside VEMI while it is open.
